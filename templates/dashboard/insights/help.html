  1. Click Add property in the menu, choose the type and whether it is for sale or rent.
  2. Fill in the price, location and details, and add at least one photo (the first photo becomes the cover).
  3. Click Save. {% if user.is_admin_role %}Then click Publish on website.{% else %}An administrator publishes it after checking it (unless you are allowed to publish directly).{% endif %}
  4. Owners can also submit properties from the website ("Submit your property"); they appear under Unassigned submissions for an admin to give to an agent.

  • When a client sends a message from a listing, you get an email and a notification, and the client is taken to your WhatsApp with the listing's link.
  • Clients can also write in the Agent replies chat box on the website. Answer from Inquiries: your reply appears in their chat box straight away.
  • Each inquiry has a Chat on WhatsApp button that opens a chat with the client, including the listing's link.
  • Conversations are private: the client sees them only on the phone or computer they used. They are deleted {{ retention_days }} days after the last message.
  • Never ask a client to pay before they have visited the property.

  • After {{ check_days }} days, the agent (or the admins, if there is no agent) gets a reminder with how many clients reached out.
  • Repost if it is still on offer: it comes back to the top as new. Mark as sold / rented if it is taken. Remove from website if it should not be shown any more.
  • See them all under All properties / My listings → Online over a month.
{% if user.is_admin_role %}

Open a published listing, find the Hot deal box, choose how long (or an end date) and click Make it a hot deal. It is shown first on the website until then, whenever it was posted. The price is never changed.

  • Active users: admins and agents online now and when each was last seen, plus visitors on the website now (device and page).
  • Analytics: visitors and pages viewed per day, devices, where visitors arrive, and how many messaged an agent.
  • Reports: every figure about listings, clients, agents and payments, with downloads for Excel.
  • Problem reports: fake listings, wrong information, agent problems and website problems reported by visitors or staff. Mark each one resolved or dismissed, with a note.
{% endif %}

Keep contracts, title deeds, IDs and forms under Documents (PDF, Word, Excel or photos, up to 10 MB). They are private and never shown on the website. {% if user.is_admin_role %}Choose "Admins and agents" to share a document with agents: they see it only if it is general or about one of their listings.{% else %}You can upload documents for your own listings; administrators can see them too.{% endif %}

Sign in from the Sign In link at the very bottom of the website. A 6-digit code is emailed to you each time. Never share your password or code; our team will never ask for it. Forgot your password? Use "Forgot password" on the sign-in page.
